Leaflet Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026 · Effective: July 1, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile application Leaflet: Plant Care (hereinafter "Leaflet" or "the app") collects, uses, shares, and protects personal data belonging to its users.

1. Data Controller

App Owner: MFK Apps Studio
Contact: appsmfk@gmail.com

2. Data Collected

2.1 Automatically Collected Data (sent to our servers)

  • Anonymous device identifiers (Firebase Analytics anonymous ID and an anonymous, on-device generated user ID)
  • Advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID / IDFA — user-resettable)
  • Anonymous usage statistics (which screens are visited, counts of actions such as adding or watering a plant, ad impressions and purchase events — plant names, photos, and notes are never sent)

2.2 Data Processed Locally (Never Leaves the Device)

Leaflet is privacy-first; your plant data never leaves your device:

  • Plants: Plant name/species, photos you add, notes, watering and fertilizing intervals, and last-care dates — stored on device only.
  • Reminders: Watering reminder times and schedule — on device only.
  • Settings and preferences: Theme (light/dark), language, reminder time, unlocked theme packs, and premium status — on device only (local database and preference store).

Leaflet does not require you to create an account; your name, email, or location is not collected.

3. Purpose of Use

  • Providing app functionality (managing plants, reminders, and photos on device)
  • Ad display (free users only, via AdMob)
  • Anonymous usage statistics for product development
  • Managing premium purchases and restore operations

4. Third-Party Services

The following services are used:

  • Google Firebase Analytics (anonymous usage statistics) — Privacy policy
  • Google AdMob (interstitial, rewarded, and app-open ads) — Privacy policy
  • Apple App Store and Google Play Billing (premium purchases) — Apple · Google

No other third-party services (social SDKs, attribution services, etc.) are used. There is no server-side user account or cloud database.

5. Advertising & Consent (UMP / ATT)

The free tier of Leaflet shows ads via Google AdMob (interstitial, rewarded, and app-open ads — there are no banner ads). Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, and Switzerland are shown a consent screen via Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). On iOS an App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt is also shown; if you do not grant permission, you are served non-personalized ads.

You can also disable personalization in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads · iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking). Rewarded ads (e.g. to unlock a theme pack) are fully optional. By purchasing Premium you can remove all ads — for premium users the ad SDK is never initialized.

6. Permissions

  • Notifications: Watering reminders.
  • Camera / Photos: Optionally add photos to your plants (photos are stored on device only).
  • Internet: Ads, premium purchase verification, and anonymous analytics.

7. Data Security

  • All internet traffic is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS
  • Your plant data (plants, photos, notes, reminders, settings) never leaves your device
  • Premium payments are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play; no card data reaches Leaflet
  • Anonymous analytics data is auto-deleted after 14 months (Firebase Analytics retention policy)

8. User Rights (KVKK / GDPR)

Under KVKK Article 11 and GDPR Articles 15–22, you have the right to:

  • Know whether your data is being processed
  • Access your data
  • Request correction, deletion, or restriction of processing
  • Object to automated decision-making
  • Data portability

Since all your plant data is stored on your device, you can permanently delete it by deleting a plant or uninstalling the app. For deletion requests regarding anonymous analytics data, please contact appsmfk@gmail.com.

9. Children's Privacy

Leaflet does not knowingly collect data from users under 13. If you are under 13, please do not use the app.

10. Changes

We reserve the right to modify this privacy policy. Material changes will be announced via in-app notification. The current version is always published on this page.

11. Contact

For questions: appsmfk@gmail.com
